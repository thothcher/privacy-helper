# Privacy Helper

**Terms & Privacy, Decoded**: an interactive 13-slide lesson for front-end developers on Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policies, with the rules in the EU, the USA and Georgia.

**Live:** https://thothcher.github.io/privacy-helper/

## What's inside

- **Part A · Terms & Conditions** (slides 2–6): agreement styles, the 12 standard clauses, regional rules, a consent-record demo, and what goes wrong without terms.
- **Part B · Privacy Policy** (slides 7–11): personal-data quiz, a 12-section policy audit, GDPR / CCPA / Georgian law, a cookie-banner lab, and a "real or fake site" game.
- **Wrap-up** (slide 12): recap, homework and a final agreement.
- **Bonus · AI prompt kit** (slide 13): fill in your brand's details, copy one prompt, and paste it into any AI assistant to get a personalised Privacy Policy and/or Terms draft built from every section in the lesson. Your entries are saved in your browser.

## Controls

`←` `→` or `Space` to move · `N` teacher notes · `O` all slides · `F` fullscreen. Link to a slide with `#s1` … `#s13`.

## Notes

- One self-contained HTML file. Fonts come from Google Fonts and photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), so an internet connection is needed for those.
- Educational overview, not legal advice. Have AI-generated drafts reviewed by a qualified lawyer.
